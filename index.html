      About the Research

      This platform is designed to support the academic thesis titled:
      "Predicting Digital Eye Strain Factors: A Cross-Sectional Study Based on Behavioral and Ergonomic Data".

      With the increasing shift towards online learning and digital coursework, university students spend long hours staring at computer and smartphone screens. This research investigates the statistical associations between daily screen duration, ergonomic viewing habits, break schedules, ambient lighting, and reported Digital Eye Strain (DES) symptoms.

      Target Calculation & Leakage Prevention

      To avoid target leakage, input features for model predictions consist strictly of Demographic and Ergonomic Section A variables (Q1-Q12). Symptom Section B questions (Q13-Q19) are used exclusively to compute the ground-truth outcome metric during ML model training.
